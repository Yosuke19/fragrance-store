OUR STORY

Good fragrance should be explored, not rushed.

Notesdecoeur began with a simple idea: make extraordinary fragrance easier to live with. We source authentic bottles from trusted suppliers and carefully decant each scent into travel-ready glass atomizers.

Every order is prepared by hand in small batches, labeled clearly, and packaged to arrive beautifully. Because finding your signature should feel personal—not overwhelming.

“Wear it through your day.
Let the fragrance find you.”
